A Snake game with a twist, each item you eat launch a mini game. Succeed the mini game to gain a point and grow the snake, if you fail, you lose a life. The bigger your snake is, the faster it moves and the harder are the mini games.
The mini games are:
- SHOOT: Red: Stop the cursor in the green zone to win
- DRIBBLE: Yellow: Press the key/button shown on screen in time. Do it 3 times to succeed
- PUSH: Orange: You have 5 seconds to fill the bar by mashing your key/button
- MEMORIZE: Pink: Memorize the keys/buttons shown on screen. When you hear the whistle, reproduce the sequence to win
- CELEBRATE: Purple: A list of keys/buttons is shown on screen, press them in the right order fast enough to win

Controls:

- Main menu:
 - Keyboard: Press Enter or Click to play; Press Escape to quit the game
 - Gamepad: Press A (South Button) to play; Press B (East Button) to quit the game

- Arena (Game of Snake):
  - Keyboard: Navigate with arrow keys or WASD
  - Gamepad: Navigate with D-Pad

- Mini game "SHOOT":
  - Keyboard: Press Enter or Spacebar to stop the cursor
  - Gamepad: Press A (South Button) to stop the cursor
 
- Mini game "DRIBBLE":
  - Keyboard: Play with the Arrow keys
  - Gamepad: Play with A/B/X/Y

- Mini game "PUSH":
  - Keyboard: Press Enter or Spacebar to fill the bar
  - Gamepad: Press A (South Button) to fill the bar
 
- Mini game "MEMORIZE":
  - Keyboard: Play with the Arrow keys
  - Gamepad: Play with A/B/X/Y
 
- Mini game "CELEBRATE":
  - Keyboard: Play with the Arrow keys
  - Gamepad: Play with A/B/X/Y
